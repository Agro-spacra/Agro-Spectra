# 🌾 AgroSpectra | Remote-Sensing Platform

> **"From Satellite Orbit to the Farmer's Field: Securing Tomorrow's Food Today"**

---

## 📌 Project Overview
**AgroSpectra** is an intelligent remote-sensing platform that integrates and analyzes open climate and geospatial data from **NASA Open Data**. The platform builds an advanced early-warning system to monitor soil moisture and vegetation health changes, optimizing water consumption, protecting crops from flash droughts and agricultural pests, and supporting environmental sustainability and food security.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Impact & Food Security
The project aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), Saudi Vision 2030, and the Saudi Green Initiative to empower sustainable agriculture and water security:
* **30% Water Irrigation Conservation:** Achieved through precise determination of actual soil water requirements.
* **Early Warning for Droughts & Pests:** Providing simplified color-coded alerts (Green/Yellow/Red) for farmers and decision-makers before crises escalate.
* **Human & Local Empowerment:** Translating complex satellite data analytics into direct, actionable, and user-friendly insights for local farmers.

---

## 🛰️ NASA Data Integration
AgroSpectra's model relies on the integration of multiple open NASA data sources:
1. **NASA POWER API:** Extracting soil moisture indices, temperature, and solar radiation parameters.
2. **MODIS / VIIRS Data:** Tracking Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for vegetation health and monitoring geospatial crop changes.
